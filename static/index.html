Welcome to EditFlow

Your professional video processing workflow tool. Let's get you started!

🎬

Add Your Videos

Drop video files or click the browse button to select your footage. Drag to reorder clips as needed.

🎨

Create Branding

Go to the Branding tab to set up your intro, outro, and subscribe popup animations. Reference files directly - no copying needed!

⚙️

Configure Settings

Choose transitions, quality presets, and encoder. NVENC gives you blazing fast GPU encoding if you have an NVIDIA card!

🚀

Process & Export

Hit "Process Videos" and watch the magic happen. Your finished video will appear in the Outputs tab!
